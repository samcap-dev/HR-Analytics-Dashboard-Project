# 👩‍💼 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes **employee attendance, work-from-home (WFH) adoption, and sick leave trends**.  
It provides HR teams with **actionable workforce insights** to improve policies, manage absenteeism, and ensure higher employee engagement and retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Track **employee presence, WFH, and sick leave trends** over time.  
- Provide **department-level** and **employee-level attendance analysis**.  
- Detect absenteeism patterns for **better workforce management**.  
- Enable HR managers with **self-service reporting** to explore workforce data.  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- 📊 **Presence vs Absence** → Tracks employee attendance rates.  
- 🏢 **Department-Wise Analysis** → Attendance, WFH, and sick leave by department.  
- 👨‍💼 **Employee Drill-Down** → Insights at an individual employee level.  
- 📅 **Time-Series Analysis** → Monthly and quarterly breakdown of attendance.  
- ⚙️ **Custom DAX Metrics**:  
  - Total Working Days  
  - Presence Days  
  - WFH Days  
  - Sick Leave Days  

---

## 🛠️ Skills & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** → Interactive reporting.  
- **Power Query** → Data cleaning and integration of attendance logs.  
- **DAX** → Calculations for WFH days, Sick leave, Total working days.  
- **Time-Series Visualization** → Trend charts, line graphs, and heatmaps.  
- **Workforce Analytics** → Absenteeism patterns and HR decision support.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file.  
2. Open it in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Filter by month, department, or employee to explore trends.  


---

## 📢 Key Insights
- **Marketing and Sales** teams had higher WFH adoption compared to other departments.  
- Sick leave peaked in **winter months (Dec–Jan)**, likely due to seasonal flu.  
- A small group of employees showed **repeated absenteeism**, requiring HR intervention.  
- Overall **presence rate stayed above 85%**, reflecting healthy workforce engagement.
